Rules and eligibility

Who can enter

  • A DUPR ID and rating are required for every player, in every category.
  • Every player must be rated 3.7 or below.
  • Open Singles and Open Doubles are open to all genders.

How entry works

  • Registration closes Saturday 28 November, 11:59 PM IST.
  • Doubles: the captain enters both players' names, DUPR IDs and ratings, plus the partner's phone number and their own phone number and email, and pays ₹1,599 for the team.
  • No partner? Request one for free. Pairing is first-come, first-served and isn't guaranteed. If a suitable partner is found we'll contact you.
  • Once you're paired and you both agree, each of you pays ₹800 — requested only after pairing is confirmed.
  • A pairing request does not guarantee a tournament slot. You can also register for Open Singles separately.
  • A full category has a free waitlist. Joining doesn't hold a spot. Admins may increase capacity or invite selected waitlisted entries.

Good to know

Is a DUPR ID mandatory?
Yes — for every player, in every category. Enter each player's DUPR ID and current rating; everyone must be rated 3.7 or below. No DUPR account yet? Creating one is free at dupr.com.
How does the Open Doubles entry work?
The captain registers and pays ₹1,599 for the whole team, entering both players' names, DUPR IDs and ratings. The captain's phone number and email and the partner's phone number are required.
I don't have a partner. What can I do?
Request a partner for free. Pairing is first-come, first-served and isn't guaranteed. We'll contact you if a suitable partner is found. Once you're paired and you both agree, each of you pays ₹800 — requested only after pairing is confirmed. A pairing request does not guarantee a tournament slot.
What if my category is full?
Join the waitlist for free. It doesn't hold a spot — if one opens, we'll message you with a payment link.
What do winners receive?
Medals, trophies and cash prize cheques for winners and runners-up, from a ₹30,000 total prize pool. The breakdown will be announced later.
